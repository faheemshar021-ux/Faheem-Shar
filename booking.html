Before you ask

Good to know.

How far in advance should I book?

For peak mountain season, eight to twelve weeks is ideal. For city escapes, we can often plan something lovely with less notice.

Can I customise a package?

Absolutely. Packages are starting points, not rules. Tell us your pace, interests, and must-see moments.

Are flights included?

International flights are not included, but we can advise on routes and coordinate all transfers once you arrive.

What group sizes do you work with?

Our shared trips stay intimate at 6 to 12 guests. Private journeys can be arranged for couples, families, and friends.
